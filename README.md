## 1. About Elite Wear

Elite Wear is a small clothing shop that provides affordable and
fashionable clothing for women, men, and children.

The shop was opened in 2026 to provide stylish and quality clothing
at affordable prices.

### Mission

To deliver fashionable, affordable, and high-quality clothing with
excellent customer service.

### Vision

To become one of the most trusted clothing stores in the local
community and grow online.

---

## 2. Target Audience

The Elite Wear website is designed for:

- Teenagers
- Adults
- Customers looking for affordable fashion

---

## 3. Website Goals

The main goals of the Elite Wear website are:

- Promote new clothing products
- Increase online sales
- Help customers contact the store easily
- Allow customers to view products online

## 4. Website Pages

The website contains five main HTML pages.

### Home Page

**File:** `index.html`

The home page introduces Elite Wear and provides links to the
different sections of the website.

It includes:

- Elite Wear introduction
- Clothing categories
- Dresses
- Shirts
- Shoes
- Navigation buttons

---

### About Us Page

**File:** `about.html`

The About Us page provides information about Elite Wear.

It includes:

- History of the organisation
- Mission
- Vision
- Target audience
- Business goals

---

### Services Page

**File:** `services.html`

The Services page displays the products and services offered by
Elite Wear.

It includes:

- Dresses
- Shirts
- Shoes
- Online shopping
- Product search
- Customer enquiries

---

### Contact Page

**File:** `contact.html`

The Contact page provides information that customers can use to
contact or visit Elite Wear.

### Address

76 Durban Street  
Port Elizabeth

### Telephone

0866724895

---

### Enquiry Page

**File:** `enquiry.html`

The Enquiry page allows customers to send an enquiry to Elite Wear.

The form includes:

- Full name
- Email address
- Product category
- Customer enquiry message
- Submit button

JavaScript is used to display a confirmation message after the
form is submitted.
