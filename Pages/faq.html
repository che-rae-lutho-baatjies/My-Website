Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information for Elite Wear customers.

What products do you offer?

Elite Wear offers dresses, shirts and tops, pants and footwear for different styles and occasions.

Can I ask about product availability?

Yes. Customers can use the enquiry page or contact Elite Wear by phone for product questions.

Can I create a customer account?

The customer login interface is included in the project. Full account functionality is planned for a future version.

Does the website have a shopping cart?

Yes. A front-end shopping cart page has been added to demonstrate the planned shopping experience.

How do I contact Elite Wear?

Visit 76 Durban Street, Port Elizabeth, or call 0866724895 for general enquiries.

How can I make a product enquiry?

Open the Enquiry page, choose a category, provide your details and submit your question.
