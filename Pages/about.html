About ELITE WEAR

Fashion that fits your style, lifestyle and budget.

Our Story

Elite Wear is a local clothing business established in 2026 to provide fashionable and affordable clothing for women, men and children.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make everyday fashion more accessible by offering quality clothing, useful product information and friendly customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local fashion brand and expand our online presence while continuing to provide convenient access to clothing and footwear.

Our Values

  • Quality and value for money
  • Friendly and respectful customer service
  • Fashion variety and personal expression
  • Convenient communication with customers

SMART Business Goals

  • Increase monthly website visitors from the launch baseline to 5,000 by month 4.
  • Increase online and enquiry-generated sales by 15% within six months from the launch baseline.
  • Achieve at least 100 qualified product enquiries per month by month 6, measured from the website enquiry form.
  • Maintain at least a 90% successful page-navigation rate during usability testing by the end of the seven-month project.
